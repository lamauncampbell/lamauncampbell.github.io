Cybersecurity & Privacy Risk Research Lead

UDC AMIE/BEYA Engineering Conference Team, Washington, DC. 2026.

Our team designed a child safety tracking device. I led the privacy and security side: what the device knows, who is allowed to see it, and how to stop it being turned against the child it protects.

Misuse scenario

Safeguard

Unauthorized trackingSomeone who is not a guardian tries to follow the child's location.

Verified accessLocation is released only to guardians the system has verified.

InterceptionLocation is read while it travels from the device to the system.

EncryptionData is encrypted before it crosses the trust boundary.

Over-collectionThe system gathers or keeps more than it needs.

Data minimizationThe policy gate sends the least data possible, only when it is needed.

  • Led the privacy and security architecture analysis for the device design, presented at the AMIE/BEYA Conference.
  • Defined trust boundaries and data flow at the device and system levels so data was handled responsibly and stayed compliant.
  • Wrote data minimization policies that decide when, and how, information is allowed to leave the device.
  • Worked through misuse scenarios such as unauthorized tracking and proposed technical safeguards against each one.
